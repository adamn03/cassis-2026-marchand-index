# Tier-1 pilot results — The Marchand Index (pilot2)

Generated by `pilot2/compute_oaq.py`. Method locked in `pilot2/preregistration.md` (§4,6,7,8,9,10,11; A1-A5). All effects reported with 95% bootstrap CI regardless of direction.

**A5 headline market correction:** `OAQ_portable = engagement_raw − λ × max(0, market_z) − peer_mean` with **λ = 0.5** (maximum-entropy midpoint between 0 and 1; sensitivity ladder reported below). Locked-v1 (two-sided, λ=1) retained in `OAQ_portable_lockedv1` for audit.

## Configuration

- N skaters: 160 (forwards 96, defense 64)
- K peers: 10 (Mahalanobis, within-group inverse covariance of standardized (age, ppg, toi_per_game))
- Bootstrap draws: 1000, seed 20260526
- Market proxy components used: metro_population, arena_attendance
- Reddit availability: 160/160 players had non-NULL Reddit data
- match_quality=low: 1 (identity-resolution); cap_quality=low: 1 (excluded from MI leaderboard)
- **A4 denominator:** marchand_index uses `expected_cap` (per-group OLS of cap_hit_M ~ PPG + TOI/G, age excluded, floored at $0.775M). `marchand_index_rawcap` (§8-original, raw cap denominator) and `marchand_index_hybrid` (rookies → expected_cap, others → raw cap) are also computed for the 5-lens leaderboard comparison.
- **Rookie-deal flag:** cap ≤ $0.975M AND age ≤ 25 → 27 of 160 players.
